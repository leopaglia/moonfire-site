Support

Questions, problems or feedback about Moonfire? Write to wholeheartedfaith.app@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Moonfire. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Install the app, finish the setup questions and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen, or in Settings inside the app. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Everything you do in the app is stored only on your phone, so it doesn't move to a new device.

I'm not getting my reminder.

Check that notifications for Moonfire are on in iOS Settings, and that the reminder is on in the app's Settings.

Is what I write to burn saved anywhere?

No. What you write to let go of is never stored or sent: it exists only on the screen until it burns. Your intentions are kept only on your phone, in Your sky.

I missed a night. What happens?

Your 30 nights continue where you left off; only your streak resets. If you start the 30 nights over, the stars you earned stay in your sky as a constellation.
