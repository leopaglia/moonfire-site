Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what Moonfire (“the app”, “we”) collects and why. The short version: there are no accounts, your answers stay on your phone, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers to the setup questions, such as your first name if you give it, your date of birth (used only to find your sun sign), what you want to let go of and your reminder time.
  • Your progress, your constellations, your streaks and the intentions you write.

What you write to burn is never saved anywhere, not even on your device: it exists only on the screen until it burns.

This information is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, price, dates). It does not receive your name, email or payment details. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

App usage. We collect anonymous usage events, such as finishing a setup step, viewing the subscription screen or starting a trial, to understand how the app is used and improve it. These events are not linked to your name or contact details. Provider: PostHog, privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, your birthday or anything you write in the app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app’s settings or in iOS Settings.

Tracking and advertising

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not show ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can stop all collection by deleting the app. To ask about the anonymous data held by our providers, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
